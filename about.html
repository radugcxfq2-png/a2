About Spicely

Twelve fresh jars beat forty forgotten ones.

Why spices

A pinch of the right spice costs almost nothing and changes a dish completely. Yet most spice racks are full of jars bought for one recipe and never opened again. Spicely exists to help you use the ones you have, and to buy fewer, better ones.

How we work

Our guides come from everyday cooking, cross-checked with published guidance on dried food storage and with what spice growers and millers say about their own products. A second person reads each guide before it is published.

What to expect

No shop, no sponsored jars and no health claims. Spices are for flavour. If you spot an error, tell us through the contact page and we will correct it. We are based at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
